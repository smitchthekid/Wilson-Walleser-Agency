# What is Content Marketing? And How Does it Outperform Paid Advertising?

# **56 words 376 characters**

**Here are 56 words and 376 characters that won't help you understand the meaning of content marketing.**

According to The Content Marketing Institute, “Content marketing’s purpose is to attract and retain customers by consistently creating and curating relevant and valuable content with the intention of changing or enhancing consumer behavior. It is an ongoing process that is best integrated into your overall marketing strategy, and it focuses on owning media, not renting it.”

## The only 7 words you need to know: "focus on owning media, not renting it".

Virtually every type of advertising and marketing falls within these two categories: owned or rented. Every form of paid media is a "rent" strategy. You pay for this, you get this, see you again next month.

Not unlike renting an apartment or leasing a car, paid advertising has advantages. Savvy advertisers get results faster, keep cash liquid, and the budget remains flexible in the sway of changing demand.

## **Content Marketing: Do You Want to Rent or Own Your Business Strategy?**

The difference between renting houses and cars versus paid media: in the former case you know exactly what you're getting. Results are guaranteed. You don't have to rely on someone else to measure the results for you.

There are three ways to get what you want in this world: buy it, earn it, or steal it.

## Playing the Long Game - Long Term Marketing

Every golf course has "that guy" with a $700 driver who can't putt for shit? That' guy probably works in advertising. It's easy to throw money at the wrong problems.

### How Long Does It Take for Content Marketing to Work?

Content marketing is a long term approach to earned media. In most cases, we can help businesses rank their websites, grow local search rankings, and **start delivering results in 3 - 6 months**.

It's an semi-autonomous process throughout, that takes time and patience. While creating some content and building search engine credibility is important, power movers build audiences faster by focusing on search data, understanding key business value, and creating technically sound search engine programs.

## Content Value Grows Over Time

Content value matures over time. Just like anything it has a life cycle. Some blogs and articles lose value over time and become obsolete. But, well-crafted content strategies can deliver results for well over a decade.

## **Inbound Lead Generation**

When we use informational content to drive online leads or checkouts, we call this inbound marketing. The benefit of inbound lead generation is that sales teams spend much less time prospecting. Instead of endless cold calls or buying contact lists, customers come to you. Inbound leads are tracked by evaluating which online sources drove inbound phone calls, emails, request-for-quotes (RFQ) or request-for-pricing (RFP).

## **Provides Shareable Content for Social Media Sharing**

Social media is a powerful platform for distributing branded content. Brands often get it wrong when they forget that social media is called "social" "media" for a reason. It's supposed to be a platform for socializing, not spamming hard sales and product information. User guides, pro tips, life hacks, and educational articles are great ways to inspire engagement from both new and existing customers.

## **Reduce Customer Acquisition Costs**

Customer acquisition costs are a [killer for startups and small businesses](https://www.forentrepreneurs.com/startup-killer/). The major advantage of writing content on the web, as opposed to paid advertising, content is forever. Instead of paying for each click to your website in pay-per-click advertising, ([*PPC strategies*](https://pleasecart.com/2020/02/19/advertising/the-truth-about-google-advertising-differences-between-google-facebook-advertising-strategies/)), a single article may have a lifetime of up to 10 years, sometimes even longer. If you invest in quality content early, you can often see the fruit of your labor in just a few short months as your articles rise to prominence in search engines.

## **Guide Customers through Their Buying Journey**

Potential customers shop and read online at [every stage of the buying process](https://kapost.com/b/6-different-types-of-buyer-journey-maps/). In fact, [B2B buyers lean on digital information more than ever](https://pleasecart.com/2019/11/15/seo-product-marketing/how-much-influence-does-the-internet-have-on-customer-purchases/).

**It doesn't matter if it's toilet paper or critical hardware, buyers look to the internet when they need basic evaluations or justification for the cost and value of a purchase.**

Despite the marketing differences between business to business (B2B) and business-to-consumer (B2B) activity, B2B buyers often make purchase decisions for products they will not use themselves. Meaning, they need additional information to justify purchase orders for capital purchases.

## **Help B2B Buyers Justify Large Purchases**

Content marketing is a great way to reach customers at the beginning of a purchase decision but also at the end.

For example, John wants to purchase a new piece of software. While software A is more expensive than software B and C, John sees that the features and capabilities will deliver cost savings and value over the long term.

John doesn't know exactly how much money he can save the company by choosing the more expensive software A. This is information senior executives want.

Content marketing is a great way for Software A to differentiate itself from lower cost competitors. For example, field-proven data and case studies on how similar companies have simplified integration, reduced training costs, and returned their investment faster by opting for Software A.

## **Reduce Customer Retention Costs**

Customers ask the same questions over and over. It's time consuming. Sometimes the answer is complex and requires trouble shooting with the customer to diagnose the problem, especially in technology industries or manufacturing.

Creating content FAQs or user guides reduce customer hand-holding, reduce inbound clutter, and help customers understand key features and proper product usage from the beginning to the end of the product life cycle.

## **Owning Your Data and Digital Retail Space**

One of the most important ideas within content marketing is pushing your audience toward your own sovereign domain. Longevity and sustainable competitive advantage will continue to be based around creating a central hub that allows people to find everything you have to offer in a few clicks.

Owning your own webpage means having access to an immense amount of information and tools that would otherwise go unnoticed such as the effectiveness of your web pages, direct interaction with readers, and power to monetize your presence on the web.

## **Sell More by Selling Less**

Content marketing **is not** a sales pitch. It is not the time to shout out at your customers while juggling knives. "Look at me, I'm awesome, don't you want to be awesome?!"

Content marketing **is** a method that helps customers make educated purchase decisions. When done right, you develop an inner voice with your customers and build long-lasting, sustainable business relationships.

Content marketing **is not** a short term approach: it mirrors the building of relationships in the real world: create trust over time, be transparent, connect over a mutual goal or understanding.

Imagine the difference between trying to sell products or services to people you know versus random people off the street. People are exponentially more likely to buy things from sellers that they trust and understand.

## **Content Marketing Is A Spectator Sport**

Creating great content just doesn’t mean producing information that people enjoyed and found valuable. Valuable and measurable content campaigns require a foundation of thought that inspires action, something that many businesses struggle to define or tailor within the box of their business model.

## **Content Builds Customer Belief Systems**

Like all movements of human expression, businesses also influence culture, world view, and personal beliefs. Content marketing has the same ability to move mountains within the hearts and minds of its consumers. Sometimes it's a goofy video or documenting the day-to-day operations of your organization. Other times, its the deep, dark, and technical -- the nuts and bolts that help smart customers make a decision and get on with their life.

Great content marketers get people to “sing-along’ and spread their message to the world in a viral way. By getting others to deliver your message for you, people stop feeling like they are being sold on something, and starting feeling like they are becoming a part of something.

**Content is just content unless it’s driving behavioral change**

# Strategies for Product Advertising Campaigns

Advertising strategies differ between low, mid, and high-end products. One must not only understand the nature of their customers, but also the nature of their competition.

## What Creates Competition in Digital Markets and PPC Advertising?

Search volume is the most indicative metric for online competition. But, search volume is easily confused with search intent. There's a difference between people who want to learn more about a product and people who want to buy a product. Placing your ad in front of the wrong volume for high-volume search queries is not only unfruitful but also expensive.

## High Competition Markets

Competition is steep in markets with high visibility brands, novel or well-recognized products, and high margin sales. Naturally, products with a large profit margin, regardless of price, will inevitably drive the price of advertising upwards. A popular brand means competitors are numerous and quite aggressive in their bidding strategies.

### Branded Search Terms

Brand search term advertising means that keywords include certain trademarks and namesakes. For example, the search includes the name of a company, product line, patent, or registered trademark.

The line between a branded and non-branded search is often gray. For example, the difference between searching for "Kleenex" and "tissue paper". In the midwest, we don't call paper for blowing your nose 'tissue paper', we call it Kleenex, even though we are actually referring to a single brand of tissue paper.

**Brands don't matter If you have boogers on your face, you'll happily take snot tissue.**

Accessibility is a premium feature zero alternatives. alternative options equal zero.

Branded searches speak  . You'll also understand where your competitors are throwing money at the wall even when it won't stick. Great advertising requires picking up the dollars your competitors leave behind but also knowing how to walk away from nickel and dime customers.

Customers like shopping for cheaper and more valuable options. Do not forget that customer curiosity is at your expense. Budgets pay for ad clicks, not customers.

**Some customers are brand-sensitive, others are not.**

If you're buying Gucci or Prada purse, getting an ad on the top of Google is expensive. However, there are many ways around targeting high-value buyers on low-budget strategies. You have to dig into the search data that your competitors are not looking at.

## Low Competition Google Search Advertising

When advertising competition is low, this usually means it's a new product and relatively unknown to search users. Obviously, people don't search for things they don't know about in specific ways. They start with general search terms and hopefully discover your product or business.

### Low Competition Searches: The Digital Marketing Minesweeper

**It must be said: never estimate the stupidity of big box advertisers with virtually unlimited advertising budgets.**

Sometimes, the market does not reflect a strong advertising position.

The last thing you want to do with an introductory advertising campaign is go head to head with companies 100x your budget. They will have fun bidding over and stealing your lunch money-- if you are not looking..

Interestingly, big brands often neglect bread and butter keywords-- the less obvious ones. because they don't have time. the capacity to measure and revise accordingly for ad content that works.

Big ad campaigns work by volume and margin. If we create enough ads for enough products, a technically sound advertising strategy will deliver results on adjusted profits. Meaning, even if we drag in a lot of search clicks from non-ideal customers, we'll drag in enough high-value customers to erase any losses and remain profitable on the long game.

**Advertising head-to-head with big business is not a game small business can afford to play, let alone lose.**

Most companies between $1 million to $10 million will outsource their advertising strategies if they don't have the internal capacity themselves. Or, at worst, underestimate the scope and complexity of digital market places.

Being a few dollars off in price is the difference between cashing in sales and being particularly not competitive. For products with free shipping delivered within 2 days by Amazon, it's unlikely that a business not offering those perks would receive the order.

### Realize Your Limitations Early

Advertising is sometimes regulated by search engine policies or government regulation. For example, Google refuses to serve ads for certain items such as cannabis sales, CBD products, and many nicotine products. Even worse, Google often confuses perfectly acceptable ads for various reasons: flagged as sexually inappropriate, illegal, or as not conforming to editorial guidelines.

From experience, Google uses an algorithm to vette and filter eligible ads, Google does the same for deciding which new ads will appear on its search engines.

In the most stupid cases, which occur far too often, you'll have to fight with Google just to reverse a decision for an ad that is flagged inappropriate for a reason that is completely false.

## How Small Brands Win in Big Markets

There's no trick to winning over customers. Enterprise-level advertising managers know they have a lot more budget to play with and mistakes are expected. The money they spend on mistakes each month might far exceed your budget. Successful startups and small business serve markets in a way that big brands can't: a focused effort on segments big brands neglect.,

The advantage small brands have against big brands: use a companies brand against them. That doesn't mean running smear campaigns and actively calling out your competitors. It just means putting yourself in the position to capture customers your competitors underserve.

The perfect way to target your competitors without poking the bear: start bidding wars.

For example: use search data to target customers searching for products for your competitors, even if it's amazon. I don't know how to explain this more simply: if you want cheap clicks that compete with your competitors on Amazon, you start by targeting less sought after searches with equally relevant purchase intent.

- Searches with spelling errors
- Searches for product alternatives
- Searches that include shopping destinations: near me, Amazon, local, buyer's guide, ect.
- Target low-volume keywords that included your competitors brand names and website locations
- Build short and simple blog posts that quickly target low-volume buyers for high-margin products

### If You Want to Sell Products Online, Start with the Low Hanging Fruit

One thing business owners and executives need to understand;

**Advertise everything for everyone and you'll sell nothing.**

There's a reason you hire marketing and advertising strategists: pay them to look at things you don't have time for. Most specifically, pay them to look at things you're competitors don't have time for. That's how you'll make money.
